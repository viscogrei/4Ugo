Electrical Engineering Methodology

Electrical Engineering Competence & Technical Standards

Every solar array, distribution board, and industrial power network we deploy is backed by rigorous electrical calculations, international safety codes, and calibrated test instrumentation.

01

Load Profiling & Site Survey

Clamp-meter phase logging across R-Y-B lines, inductive starting-current measurement, soil resistivity checks, and roof structural assessment.

02

AutoCAD SLD & Cable Sizing

Detailed AutoCAD Single-Line Diagrams (SLD), short-circuit fault ratings, <2% voltage-drop cable sizing, and PVsyst solar yield simulation.

03

Precision Install & Protection

Galvanized/PVC conduit dressing, custom ATS & MCCB/RCBO panels, Type 1+2 AC/DC surge arrestors, and chemical copper earth pits.

04

Megger Testing & Commissioning

500V/1000V Megger insulation resistance testing, earth loop impedance verification, BMS telemetry setup, and NEMSA inspection handover.
